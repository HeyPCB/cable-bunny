# Cable Bunny

**Hook:** Is it a charge-only cable? Ask the bunny.

**Description (60 words):**
Plug both ends of any USB-C cable into Cable Bunny's feet and boop its nose. Its ears light up for every
wire that actually works: power, ground and CC on the left, data and SBU on the right. A glowing heart
means its battery is fine. Sort that drawer of mystery cables in ten seconds each. No app, no
charging.

**Suggested price:** $15
